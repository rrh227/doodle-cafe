# 🎨 Doodle Cafe — Player Tutorial

Welcome to **Doodle Cafe**! You run a cozy little cafe where customers order food and drinks — but instead of cooking, you *doodle* their orders. Pick a base, color it, decorate it with toppings, and serve it before the customer loses patience. Keep your reputation up, or the cafe closes!

## The Basics

Each round of play is a single customer order, and it always follows the same loop:

```
Customer arrives  →  Read their prompt  →  Pick a base  →  Color it
       →  Add toppings  →  Serve!  →  Get your score  →  Next customer
```

There are no levels or days — the run is **endless**. You serve customers one after another until your reputation drops too low. Your goal is to survive as long as possible and rack up the highest total score.

---

## How to Build an Order

### Step 1 — Read the Prompt

Every customer describes what they want. Early prompts are literal ("a chocolate latte with whipped cream"), but later customers get vague and artsy ("something that sounds quiet"). Interpreting the prompt is the whole game!

### Step 2 — Pick a Base

You'll be offered **2–3 bases** to choose from (cupcake, latte, toast, smoothie bowl, or iced drink). Only one is the *ideal* pick for the prompt — choose wisely. You **must** select a base before the Serve button unlocks.

### Step 3 — Color It

Each base is divided into sections (3–4 per base). Click a color swatch (or use the free-form color picker), then click a section to paint it. Colors are scored by how *close* they are to the customer's imagined color — you don't need a pixel-perfect match, but the closer the better.

### Step 4 — Add Toppings

Drag toppings from the catalog onto your creation. Rules of the topping table:

- You can use up to **8 different topping types** per order.
- You can place **multiple copies** of the same topping.
- Toppings that fit the prompt earn points; random clutter can cost you (see [Scoring](#scoring)).

### Step 5 — Serve!

Hit the **Serve** button to submit your creation. You'll see your score, the customer's reaction, and your star rating. Serve *fast* for a speed bonus!

## Controls

Doodle Cafe is played with the mouse (desktop only):

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Select a base | Click a base option |
| Paint a section | Click a color swatch, then click the section |
| Custom color | Use the color picker input |
| Place a topping | Drag it from the catalog onto the workspace |
| Move a topping | Left-click and drag it |
| Remove a topping | Right-click it (or drag it off the workspace) |
| Submit the order | Click **Serve** |

## Scoring

Every order is scored out of **100 points**, made up of four parts:

| Component | Max Points | How It's Earned |
|---|---|---|
| 🧁 Base choice | 20 | Picking the base the customer had in mind |
| 🎨 Colors | 30 | Average closeness of your colors to the ideal palette |
| 🍒 Toppings | 40 | Each on-theme topping earns points (best toppings ≈ 10 pts each) |
| ✨ Combo bonus | 10 | +5 for each special topping *pair* that goes together |

### Penalties & Bonuses

- **Clutter penalty:** Off-theme toppings cost **-2 points each** — but your first **2** off-theme toppings are free. A little creative flair is fine; a junk pile is not.
- **Speed bonus:** Serve quickly and earn up to **+10%** extra points. The bonus scales with how much patience time is left when you serve — instant service gets the full bonus.
- The final score is capped at 100.

### Star Ratings

Your score converts to a star rating, which drives your reputation:

| Points | Rating |
|---|---|
| 80–100 | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 60–79 | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 40–59 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| 20–39 | ⭐⭐ |
| 0–19 | ⭐ |

## Customer Patience

Every customer has a patience timer. If it runs out before you serve, the order **fails automatically** — 0 points, 1 star, and an angry *"Forget it, I don't have all day!"*

Patience depends on the order's difficulty tier:

| Tier | Patience |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 90 seconds |
| Tier 2 | 80 seconds |
| Tier 3 | 75 seconds |
| Tier 4 | 70 seconds |

⚠️ **Late-game crunch:** After order 30, every tier loses an extra **10 seconds** of patience.

## Reputation & Game Over

Your cafe starts with a reputation of **3.5 out of 5.0**. Every order nudges it up or down based on the star rating:

| Stars | Reputation Change |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | +0.25 |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | +0.15 |
| ⭐⭐⭐ | -0.15 |
| ⭐⭐ | -0.35 |
| ⭐ | -0.60 |

Notice that even a 3-star order *hurts* — customers at Doodle Cafe expect excellence!

💀 **Game over when reputation drops below 2.0.** From a fresh start, that's roughly three 1-star orders or five 2-star orders in a row.

The game-over screen shows your **total score**, **orders served**, and your **best single-order rating**. There's no "win" — the run ends when the cafe's reputation does.

## Difficulty: Order Tiers

Orders get harder the longer you survive:

| Orders | What You'll See |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | All Tier 1 (easy, literal prompts) |
| 6–10 | Mostly Tier 2 |
| 11–15 | Tier 2 with some Tier 3 |
| 16–25 | Mostly Tier 3 |
| 26+ | Half Tier 4 — cryptic prompts, tight timers |

Higher tiers are harder in two ways:

- **Vaguer prompts** — Tier 1 tells you exactly what to make; Tier 4 gives you a mood.
- **Fewer good answers** — Tier 1 prompts have 10+ toppings that score well; Tier 4 prompts might have only 2–3.

---

## Tips for High Scores

1. **The base is your foundation.** A wrong base costs up to 20 points before you've even started — read the prompt carefully.
2. **Don't over-decorate.** Toppings cap at 40 points, and off-theme extras cost you after the 2-topping grace. Fewer, better-chosen toppings beat a mountain of stuff.
3. **Hunt for combos.** Certain topping pairs (think: things that naturally go together) grant +5 each, up to +10.
4. **Speed pays.** The patience bonus rewards decisive serving — up to +10% just for being quick.
5. **Close-enough colors count.** Color scoring gives partial credit, so a reasonable shade is far better than leaving sections blank while you agonize over the perfect hue.
6. **Protect your reputation.** One timed-out order (-0.60) takes three 4-star orders to recover from. If time is running low, serve *something* — a 2-star order beats a 1-star timeout.
