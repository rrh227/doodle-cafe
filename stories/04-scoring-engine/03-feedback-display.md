# Feedback Display

## Objective
Show the player their score and feedback after serving an order.

## Requirements
- Score overlay appears after pressing Serve
- Shows star rating with visual stars
- Shows point breakdown
- Brief text reaction from the customer
- Dismiss to continue to next order

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Score overlay appears on serve
- [x] Star rating displayed visually
- [x] Point breakdown shown
- [x] Customer reaction text varies by score
- [x] "Next Order" button dismisses and resets workspace
